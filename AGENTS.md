# Stop the Invasion

## Product agreement
Single-player party deckbuilder, currently Bob versus alien-possessed store security in a replayable 1v1 combat laboratory. Campaign, map/shop/boss, items (potions), collectible statuses (relics), additional party characters and co-op are outside this milestone. The approved current combat model is one persistent, single-lane encounter timeline, not a six-slot queue or a two-lane packing board.

## Alien possession and time theme
The invaders are time-manipulating aliens possessing ordinary people, not an infection turning them into monsters. Hosts retain their recognizable jobs, personalities and themed attacks: Possessed Security still checks receipts. Preserve the everyday caricatures and comic horror-comedy. The theme-only change updates naming, menu lore and encounter copy without changing artwork, stats, cards, timing or balance rules.

History is intended to become a resource through specific future abilities, not a freely editable extra hand. Reclaim (recover an actual card), Echo (temporarily replay or modify a past action) and Rewind (restore an earlier combat state) remain open design possibilities. No first mechanic has been selected or implemented; current history remains read-only. Do not implement any of these solely from the lore change without settling its rules with the user.

## Persistent timeline rules
Positions are absolute, zero-based encounter coordinates. Higher indices are farther LEFT and later; lower indices are RIGHT and earlier. Resolution consumes INCREASING indices, visually RIGHT TO LEFT. Empty positions consume time. Enemy actions stay anchored to absolute positions regardless of turn boundaries, panning, zoom, reveal/hide or ordinary card placement. The schedule continues until victory or defeat; fifty cards is not a limit.

Planning is paused with unlimited time. Bob normally advances seven positions per turn. `turnLength(state)` adds active bracket-position modifiers to the actor's persistent base and clamps the result to at least one. `turnEnd(state)` is exclusive. Extending a turn immediately reveals and opens additional positions, including any enemy actions there. The following turn begins after the last consumed position and receives a fresh movement budget: extending0–6 to0–8 makes the next normal turn9–15. Short turns intentionally accelerate resource refresh along the track. Zero-distance resource-refresh loops are forbidden.

Only positions inside `[state.position, turnEnd(state))` accept planning commands. Scouting reveals a number of POSITIONS, not cards, beyond the bracket, up to exclusive `visibleEnd(state)`. Scouted future is inspect-only. Removing the scouting buff hides those positions again even if the schedule remains cached internally. Refunding an extension or scouting card after inspecting the revealed information is an intentional free planning tactic. Hidden positions must not leak their occupancy through meshes, DOM labels, hit targets or spacing. Revealed actions are reliable unless an explicit mechanic changes or cancels them.

`CardDefinition.bracket` cards attach to the current turn bracket, never to an ordinary timing position or actor. They reserve energy and immediately change length/scouting. Removing an extension or adding shortening atomically returns out-of-range player cards to hand and releases their commitments. Card-bound attachments follow returned player hosts. Position-bound attachments and attachments bound to an enemy that leaves the current bracket return to hand and refund, even if that enemy is still scouted. Temporary bracket attachments expire at cleanup; persistent actor turn-length/scouting stats remain. Unused attachments still cost energy when resolved.

History stores independent snapshots of every consumed position, including empty and canceled positions, original action/definition, applicable attachments, signed upgrade level and events. History is read-only and never part of inventory or energy accounting. Render-only history UIDs must differ from live card UIDs. Immediate lethal stops consumption at the lethal position; all remaining commitments expire/discard during terminal cleanup without firing.

## Ordinary combat and attachments
Ordinary cards automatically choose their sole valid living target: damage attacks the enemy, self-effects apply to Bob. More than one valid choice requires an explicit target; no living target rejects placement without mutation. No actor selectors for obvious targets or attachments. Insertion previews are pure. Inserting into an occupied player position shifts toward the nearest empty non-enemy position by absolute distance; ties favor the RIGHT/lower index. Occupied queued moves rotate intervening player cards while preserving gaps and enemy anchors. Invalid/full/outside drops never mutate state. Queue reordering and depth changes snap synchronously; no spring or bounce.

Affordability uses current energy minus all commitments. Unaffordable hand cards have opaque grayscale faces and cannot start play/targeting through mouse, keyboard or touch, but remain inspectable. Zero-cost cards remain playable at zero. Refunds immediately re-enable affordable cards. Native attachment refunds remain usable inside disabled cards; an unaffordable attack may receive an affordable attachment. The energy meter has eight fixed24px slots, filled only for spendable energy, without labels or reserved patterns. The display does not change actor resource limits. Generated resources cannot finance current commitments.

Upgrade/downgrade attachments take no timeline position. Duct Tape Upgrade gives1 level to a compatible friendly card; Trip Hazard gives-1 level to a compatible enemy card. Signed levels stack without a gameplay cap and expire this turn. Costs and action identity never change. Each eligible definition has authored `scaling` increments for its own effects or bracket fields, not a universal damage bonus or percentage. Damage, Block, Exposed, healing, energy, draw and scouting floor at zero. Extension/shortening magnitudes can weaken to zero but never reverse polarity. The turn itself still has a one-position minimum. Upgrade/downgrade source cards have no scaling and cannot receive modifiers; no recursive source chains.

Card-body targets bind exact UID and follow between hand, queue and attached bracket hosts. Position targets stay at their absolute position, including empty current positions, and affect only compatible occupants. Only scalable cards with living owners are eligible; history and future remain untargetable. Upgrading an active bracket card immediately changes length/scouting and reconciles out-of-range commitments. Removing its upgrade can contract the range and refund stranded cards/position or enemy-bound attachments. Returning the bracket host to hand preserves its bound upgrades and reservations; reattaching restores them. All attachments expire into discard at cleanup, including unused hand/empty-position targets and terminal cleanup. Base definitions and enemy intents never mutate.

Hands-off resolution; dead actors never act. Block expires at the moving turn boundary. Exposed adds a flat bonus to the next incoming hit, then is consumed; stacks add. Healing caps at maximum HP without changing Block/Exposed. A hit on a pre-hit Exposed opponent is critical, using that Exposed bonus rather than another damage multiplier. Percussive Maintenance applies Ringing once per critical action. Ringing becomes active next turn, limits that actor to its first chronological action in that turn, then expires; repeated application refreshes rather than stacking duration. Bob cannot commit a second action while Ringing; attachments are not actions. Enemy actions canceled by Ringing retain their positions and are recorded in history.

Draw five, start energy two, gain two on subsequent turns, cap four; values live on actors. Each moving boundary triggers the ordinary discard/draw/energy refresh. Unplayed cards discard unless retained; resolution-drawn cards survive into the next planning hand. Played cards remain logically queued until cleanup, while presentation excludes airborne/completed UIDs. This prevents resolution-time draws from recycling cards that have not yet been included in a discard event. Seeded reshuffling/replay are deterministic. No full outcome preview.

## Architecture and ownership
- `src/game/types.ts`: shared contracts, Main owns changes.
- `src/game/content.ts`: card data and fixed absolute enemy schedule, Main owns tuning.
- `src/game/combat.ts`: deterministic combat; zero DOM/PlayCanvas imports.
- `src/view/types.ts`: scene/UI contract, Main owns changes.
- `src/view/scene.ts`, `src/view/art.ts`: physical PlayCanvas renderer and artwork.
- `src/ui.ts`, `src/style.css`: HUD, timeline camera, hit targets, planning interactions and playback.
- `scripts/balance.ts`, `scripts/balance-feedback.ts`: canonical seeded planner and diagnostic report.
- `src/main.ts`, `index.html`, package/config files: integration.

Combat API: `createCombat(seed?: number): CombatState`; `availableEnergy(state, actor?: ActorId): number`; `turnLength(state): number`; `turnEnd(state): number`; `visibleEnd(state): number`; `queueCard(state, uid, target: ActorId | null, slot: number): CommandResult`; `previewPlacement(state, uid, target, slot): QueueSlot[] | null`; `removeCard(state, slot)`; `moveCard(state, from, to)`; `retargetCard(state, slot, target)`; `resolveTurn(state): ResolutionStep[]`. Every slot argument and event `slot` is ABSOLUTE, not local to a turn. `state.position` is the current turn's absolute start. `queue` is an absolute-index cache with consumed entries nulled at cleanup; future cached intents remain unchanged. `activeSlot` is the absolute consumed position or null at cleanup. Do not rebase indices or derive timing from array length.

Attachment API: `canAttachModifier(state, uid, target): boolean`; `attachModifier(state, uid, target)`; `removeModifier(state, uid)`; `upgradeLevel(state, uid, slot: number | null): number`. The old damageModifier API is removed. Targets are `{kind:'card', uid}`, `{kind:'slot', slot}` or `{kind:'bracket'}`. Level-source (`modifier.levels`) and temporal bracket attachments are mutually exclusive. Mutating planning commands return CommandResult and mutate only on success. Preview is pure, shallow and accepts hand/queued UIDs while preserving targets. Reject invalid/dead targets, noninteger/out-of-bracket indices, wrong attachment targets and commands outside planning. Resolve preflights commitments before spending, never mutates its input, and returns independent snapshots for consumed positions plus cleanup. Nested history/actions/definitions/scaling/events/cards/attachments are independently cloned. Planning-only mutations copy affected arrays, not unused history.

`src/game/upgrades.ts` owns pure `applyUpgrade(base, level)` for CardDefinition/EnemyAction and `scaledBracket(base, level)` for frequent range queries without description allocation. Scaling description templates use `{effect:0}`, `{effect:1}`, `{positions}` (magnitude) and `{scouting}`; literal numbers such as Minimum1 remain unchanged. Missing increments leave a stat unchanged. Grade0 preserves the base description. Effective views may share unchanged base structures and must not be mutated by consumers; public resolution/history snapshots clone them independently. Grade lookup must not call turnEnd, because turnLength grades active bracket sources. Both TimelineEntry and CardVisual store `upgradeLevel`; definition/action stays the base snapshot. Apply effective effects once, never add a second flat bonus.

Draw/discard CombatEvents carry independent `cards` snapshots. Cleanup emits discard BEFORE draw, even when the same UID is discarded and immediately redrawn. Presentation consumes these events, not final-pile membership. Logs are bounded; history persists for the encounter.

## Balance gate and content
Every new card and every change to costs, effects, deck, enemy schedule, resources or timing requires a fresh canonical balance run. Art-only changes do not. `npm run balance` uses32 paired seeds per candidate/policy, writes `.balance/report.json`, and reports outcomes, opportunity-normalized use, role gaps, measured tuning candidates and uncertainty. CLI: `--seeds N --output PATH`. Fingerprint includes game rules/data/types, analysis source and the full24-position enemy cycle; prior fixed-six reports do not validate this model. Coverage fails if a catalog card is never actually played/attached. Enemy actions need behavioral proof too. CI uploads the report even on coverage failure.

Planner enumerates temporal attachments, then grades on those hosts BEFORE ordinary placements, then remaining compatible grades. It plans only the current absolute bracket and never reads hidden cached intents. Analysis projects presentation-only history to[]; playable-position opportunities exclude enemy slots. Greedy evaluates effective authored damage, never treats levels as flat damage. Coverage records actual grade target effects/bracket fields, including nondamage player and enemy targets. Scouting has no fabricated information score. Strong/tactical/greedy remain bounded one-turn heuristics, not human/optimal-play evidence. Recommendations must cite measured candidates; no automatic production tuning. Fingerprints include upgrades.ts. Expanded stat-scaling studies retain32 paired seeds and have a30-minute CI deadline rather than dropping candidates or coverage.
