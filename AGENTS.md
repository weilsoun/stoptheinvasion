# Stop the Invasion

## Product agreement
Single-player party deckbuilder, initially Bob versus infected store security in a replayable 1v1 combat laboratory. Future party of three and later two-player co-op are not this milestone. Campaign, map/shop/boss, items (potions), collectible statuses (relics), art generation, environmental rules, and menus follow combat validation.

1920x1080 design coordinates; locked 16:9 with letterboxing. Bob LEFT facing right; enemies RIGHT facing left. Comic-book horror comedy in MOREMART, a parody big-box store. Physical, lit PlayCanvas card meshes, depth layers/parallax, readable hover, direct pointer grabbing. Mouse, keyboard and touch; no physics-driven combat.

## Rules
Six fixed internal timing positions, enemy intents anchored and exact; empty positions advance. No visible position numbers or resting slot boxes. Ordinary hand cards require an explicitly chosen position. Inserting into an occupied player position shifts toward the nearest empty non-enemy position; ties favor later. Moving a queued card into an occupied position rotates intervening player cards, preserving gaps and enemy anchors. Moving into an empty position leaves other gaps unchanged. Preview and commit use the same planner; invalid/full/locked drops never mutate state.

Position-first attacks enter with target=null and reserve energy; every attack needs a valid explicit target before Resolve. Self-only actions normalize to their owner. Actor-first drop/click selects a target provisionally, then a persistent arrow selects the action's position. Escape cancels without cost. Before Resolve: remove, reorder, change target via card dots; never move enemy actions. Costs reserve current energy, not forecast gains. Hands-off resolution; immediate lethal stops battle and dead actors never act. Block expires at resolution end. Exposed adds a flat bonus to the next incoming hit, then is consumed; stacks add. Base draw five, start energy two, gain two subsequently, cap four; values live on actors. Energy resets each fight. Unplayed cards discard unless retained. Resolution-drawn cards survive to next planning in addition to the normal draw. No full outcome preview. Seeded reshuffle; replay is deterministic.

Attachment cards take NO timeline position. Drop onto a compatible attack card, or onto its owner and use the returning arrow to choose a card or position. Card-body targets bind exact UID and follow between hand/queue; outline targets bind the fixed position, including empty positions, and do not follow its occupant. Only compatible living-owner damaging cards can receive a card attachment; position attachments affect only compatible occupants. Attachments reserve energy, can be removed/refunded before Resolve, and spend once at resolution start. Signed damage changes stack and floor each damage effect at zero before Block/Exposed. All attachments expire into discard at turn end, including terminal cleanup, empty positions and unplayed hand targets. Unused attachments still cost energy. Base card/intent effects never mutate.

## Architecture and ownership
- `src/game/types.ts`: shared types, Main owns contract changes.
- `src/game/content.ts`: data and encounter tuning, Main owns during parallel build.
- `src/game/combat.ts`: combat agent owns deterministic rules, zero DOM/PlayCanvas imports.
- `src/view/types.ts`: Main owns scene/UI interface.
- `src/view/scene.ts`: scene agent owns PlayCanvas renderer and procedural original artwork.
- `src/ui.ts`, `src/style.css`: interaction agent owns HUD, hand hit targets, drag targeting, queue editing, resolution animation orchestration.
- `src/main.ts`, `index.html`, package/config files: Main owns integration.

Combat API: `createCombat(seed?: number): CombatState`; `availableEnergy(state, actor?: ActorId): number`; `queueCard(state, uid, target: ActorId | null, slot: number): CommandResult`; `previewPlacement(state, uid, target, slot): QueueSlot[] | null`; `removeCard(state, slot)`; `moveCard(state, from, to)`; `retargetCard(state, slot, target)`; `resolveTurn(state): ResolutionStep[]`. Attachment API: `canAttachModifier(state, uid, target): boolean`; `attachModifier(state, uid, target)`; `removeModifier(state, uid)`; `damageModifier(state, uid, slot: number | null): number`. Mutating planning commands return CommandResult and mutate only on success. ModifierTarget is `{kind:'card', uid}` or `{kind:'slot', slot}`; attachments hold their source card and target. Preview is pure, shallow and accepts a hand or queued UID, preserving queued targets. Explicit position is required; modifiers cannot enter the ordinary queue. Reject invalid/dead explicit action targets, invalid/noninteger indices, wrong modifier targets and commands outside planning. Resolve preflights all commitments before spending or mutation, returns independent snapshots for six positions plus cleanup, and does not mutate its input. Attachment cards/target objects are cloned too. Generated resources cannot finance commitments. Logs are bounded; Block, discard/retention, energy/draw refresh and next-turn intent installation remain deterministic.

Renderer API: `createScene(canvas): ScenePort` in src/view/scene.ts; contract in view/types.ts. CardVisual uses top-left design x/y, width/height and rotation degrees. Cards, text and target dots are textured lit meshes, not DOM card replicas. Fields include target/targets, dragged, locked and damageModifier. Enemy mesh UID is EnemyAction.uid. `getCardPose(uid)` returns actual displayed bounds/rotation for grab-offset capture; dragged poses snap without spring lag, while released/neighbour cards spring smoothly. Reduced-motion snaps positions. Queue cards stay straight. Incoming visual order controls depth: rightmost card on top at rest, hovered/held cards raised. Modified physical faces show signed change and base-to-effective attack damage, not a full damage forecast. Texture keys include definition, locked state and modifier delta. Backbuffer tracks displayed bounds with graphicsDevice.resizeCanvas; DPR capped at1.5. Bob center(410,400), guard(1500,400). Queue cards172x241 at y500, centers154px apart, first left489; hand normal y812+, hover220x308 at y742. Keep UI hit regions aligned. Scene owns background/art; UI owns HUD and accessible controls.

UI API: `mountGame(root, scene): {destroy():void}`; root fixed1920x1080, scaled by Main. The same UID moves physically between hand/queue and follows the cursor at its actual grabbed point. Queue insertion previews shift neighbours without committing and use stable geometric timing hit tests. Cards overlap left-under-right. Faint, slightly angled position silhouettes appear ONLY while selecting/placing/targeting; no visible numbers or persistent boxes. An exposed outline/bottom lip targets a fixed position independently of its card body. Modifier selection brightens eligible hand/row cards and dims/rejects incompatible ones. Actor-first modifiers return to hand selected with a persistent arrow; selecting their effect target commits directly, without a separate action position. Attachment chips identify signed effect, card/position binding and expiry; clicking removes/refunds. Card-bound chips follow UID; position chips stay put. Shared physical dot coordinates: x=0.5+(index-(count-1)/2)*CARD_TARGET_GAP, y=CARD_TARGET_Y. Dots hover/focus-highlight actors and assign ordinary queued targets or begin hand actor-first selection. DOM supplies transparent hit areas and native buttons, not visible card summaries; native buttons retain their own keyboard activation. Escape/pointercancel/outside drops clear transient state without costs. Resolve is disabled for pending arrows or missing action targets, not unused committed attachments. Preserve intent/hand/queue inspection, piles/log, outcomes/replay, touch/keyboard alternatives, reduced motion and teardown.
