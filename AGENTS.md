# Stop the Invasion

## Product agreement
Single-player party deckbuilder, initially Bob versus infected store security in a replayable 1v1 combat laboratory. Future party of three and later two-player co-op are not this milestone. Campaign, map/shop/boss, items (potions), collectible statuses (relics), art generation, environmental rules, and menus follow combat validation.

1920x1080 design coordinates; locked 16:9 with letterboxing. Bob LEFT facing right; enemies RIGHT facing left. Comic-book horror comedy in MOREMART, a parody big-box store. Physical lit PlayCanvas cards with rounded corners, readable hover, animated pickup and action emphasis. Hand is fanned; queue is a straight, pronounced staggered stack. Mouse, keyboard and touch; no physics-driven combat.

## Rules
Six fixed internal timing positions resolve RIGHT TO LEFT: index5 first, then4..0, then cleanup. Enemy intents stay anchored and exact; empty positions advance without firing. Cards to the right of an enemy act before it; cards to its left act afterward. Tiny dots indicate empty positions; no visible numbers, placement arrows or ghost outlines. Pickup eases the card's center to the cursor and follows freely; release near the nearest non-enemy point places it instantly. Resting queue positions, scale and all depth ordering update synchronously, never spring or bounce during reordering. Insertion previews remain pure and keep the chosen point visible. Inserting into an occupied player position shifts toward the nearest empty non-enemy position; distance ties favor the right. Occupied queued moves rotate intervening player cards while preserving gaps and enemy anchors. Invalid/full/outside drops never mutate state.

Ordinary cards automatically choose their sole valid living target when placed: damage attacks the only enemy, self-effects apply to Bob. No target dots/strips or actor-selection highlights for those obvious choices. More than one valid choice requires an explicit target; no living target rejects placement without mutation. Attachments require a card or position, not an action timing of their own. Optional explicit actor controls use click/keyboard selection without an arrow; direct dragging only drops to cards/positions. Unaffordable hand cards dim and disable before click, keyboard, touch, drag or actor-shortcut activation. Affordability uses current energy minus all commitments; zero-cost cards stay playable at zero. Refunds immediately re-enable affordable cards. Hand hover remains inspectable; native attachment-refund buttons remain usable inside disabled cards. An otherwise unaffordable card may still receive an affordable attachment. Energy bubbles below the hand distinguish available, reserved and empty capacity; generated energy cannot finance current commitments. Hands-off resolution; immediate lethal stops battle and dead actors never act. Block expires at resolution end. Exposed adds a flat bonus to the next incoming hit, then is consumed; stacks add. Draw five, start energy two, gain two subsequently, cap four; values live on actors. Unplayed cards discard unless retained; resolution-drawn cards survive into next planning. No full outcome preview. Seeded reshuffle and replay are deterministic.

Attachment cards take NO timeline position. Drop onto a compatible attack card or an empty timing point; an occupied card's exposed lower hit area targets its fixed position. Card-body targets bind exact UID and follow between hand/queue; position targets stay put, including empty positions. Only compatible living-owner damaging cards receive card attachments; position attachments affect only compatible occupants. Attachments reserve energy, can be removed/refunded before Resolve, and spend once at resolution start. Signed damage changes stack and floor each damage effect at zero before Block/Exposed. All attachments expire into discard at turn end, including terminal cleanup, empty positions and unplayed hand targets. Unused attachments still cost energy. Base card/intent effects never mutate.

## Architecture and ownership
- `src/game/types.ts`: shared types, Main owns contract changes.
- `src/game/content.ts`: data and encounter tuning, Main owns during parallel build.
- `src/game/combat.ts`: combat agent owns deterministic rules, zero DOM/PlayCanvas imports.
- `src/view/types.ts`: Main owns scene/UI interface.
- `src/view/scene.ts`: scene agent owns PlayCanvas renderer and procedural original artwork.
- `src/ui.ts`, `src/style.css`: interaction agent owns HUD, hand hit targets, drag targeting, queue editing, resolution animation orchestration.
- `src/main.ts`, `index.html`, package/config files: Main owns integration.

Combat API: `createCombat(seed?: number): CombatState`; `availableEnergy(state, actor?: ActorId): number`; `queueCard(state, uid, target: ActorId | null, slot: number): CommandResult`; `previewPlacement(state, uid, target, slot): QueueSlot[] | null`; `removeCard(state, slot)`; `moveCard(state, from, to)`; `retargetCard(state, slot, target)`; `resolveTurn(state): ResolutionStep[]`. Attachment API: `canAttachModifier(state, uid, target): boolean`; `attachModifier(state, uid, target)`; `removeModifier(state, uid)`; `damageModifier(state, uid, slot: number | null): number`. Mutating planning commands return CommandResult and mutate only on success. ModifierTarget is `{kind:'card', uid}` or `{kind:'slot', slot}`; attachments hold their source card and target. Preview is pure, shallow and accepts a hand or queued UID, preserving queued targets. Explicit position is required; modifiers cannot enter the ordinary queue. Reject invalid/dead explicit action targets, invalid/noninteger indices, wrong modifier targets and commands outside planning. Resolve preflights all commitments before spending or mutation, returns independent snapshots for six positions plus cleanup, and does not mutate its input. Attachment cards/target objects are cloned too. Generated resources cannot finance commitments. Logs are bounded; Block, discard/retention, energy/draw refresh and next-turn intent installation remain deterministic.

Renderer API: `createScene(canvas): ScenePort`; contract in view/types.ts. CardVisual uses top-left design x/y, dimensions and rotation. Physical lit cards/text/dots are meshes, not DOM replicas. Fields include target/targets, dragged, locked and damageModifier. `getCardPose(uid)` returns actual displayed bounds/rotation. Resting queued cards (`queued && !dragged`) snap x/y, dimensions and roll in setCards itself; their spring velocities reset. Depth is always assigned immediately, including hover/pickup/front changes, with no z spring. Hand pickup and airborne action x/y retain position springs; their dimensions ease. Reduced motion snaps all poses. Hand fans at2.3 degrees per offset; hover and held cards straighten. Queue cards stay straight, shrink2% and rise7px per card in front; rightmost is on top. Queue nominal172x241, y500, stride132, first left544. Firing cards use queued=false/dragged=false, enlarge to135%, move to their actual target and exit beyond the right edge. Alpha-aware512x768 textures have36px rounded corners; opacity maps refresh with texture changes. Texture keys include definition, locked state, modifier delta and target-strip visibility. DPR cap1.5; actor centers Bob(410,400), guard(1500,400). Hand hover280x392 at y616 ends1008; queue inspection320x448 at(1540,600). Resolve is bottom-left; energy below the hand.

UI API: `mountGame(root, scene): {destroy():void}`; fixed1920x1080 root scaled by Main. Same UID is preserved through hand, queue and flight. Pointer-held cards animate to cursor center; release near a non-enemy point snaps the queue immediately. Transparent timing hit regions support keyboard focus and card-versus-position attachment selection, without painted outlines. During resolution, consume supplied descending snapshots: lift the rightmost action150ms, travel to its actual target360ms, adopt its effect snapshot and play events at impact, hold130ms, then slide off-right380ms and remove it before the next action. Reduced motion snaps the stages. Empty/canceled actions do not fire. presentedQueue excludes airborne/completed UIDs from both meshes and hit regions without mutating combat snapshots. Next-turn cleanup clears exclusions so redrawn cards can reappear. Outcome overlays wait until playback completes. Every wait checks sequence/destroy guards; restart and teardown cancel timers and resolve pending waits. Unaffordable hand cards dim and cannot start targeting; affordable attachment recipients and native refund controls remain usable. Energy bubbles distinguish available/reserved/empty capacity and update immediately on commitments/refunds. Preserve hover inspection, piles/log, replay, keyboard/touch and reduced motion.
